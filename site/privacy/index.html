Orchestrate

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 5, 2026

Orchestrate is a desktop app that runs on your own computer. There is no Orchestrate account, no Orchestrate server, and no analytics or telemetry. Your data goes between your computer and the services you connect, and never to us.

This policy covers the Orchestrate desktop app and this website, orchestrates.dev. Orchestrate is free, open-source software; its source code is public on GitHub, so anything described here can be checked there.

What Orchestrate does

Orchestrate runs and monitors Claude Code agents in your Git projects. It also has optional Mail and Calendar spaces that show your email and calendars beside your agents. Mail and Calendar only do anything once you connect an account to them.

Google user data

If you connect a Google account, Orchestrate asks Google for the following access, and uses it only as described:

Gmail (https://mail.google.com/)

  • To show your mail: list your folders, and read and search your messages.
  • To archive messages, and mark them read or unread, when you ask it to.
  • Orchestrate does not send email.

Google Calendar (calendar.readonly, calendar.events)

  • To show your calendars and their events.
  • To create, change, cancel or answer events when you ask it to. When an event has guests, Google emails them about the change, as it would if you made it in Google Calendar.

Your email address (openid, email)

  • To know which account you signed in to, so it's saved under the right address.

Where it's kept

Everything stays on your computer. Orchestrate keeps the sign-in token Google gives it in a file that only your user account on the computer can read, and the short-lived access tokens only in memory. To work quickly and offline, it keeps a cache of your mail and calendars in its data folder, also on your computer. Removing an account in Orchestrate deletes its token and its cache. Your mail and calendars themselves stay with Google and are never deleted by removing the account.

You can also withdraw Orchestrate's access at any time from your Google Account's third-party connections.

Who it's shared with

Orchestrate talks to Google directly from your computer. We never receive, store or see your Google data, and we don't sell it, share it, or use it for advertising. No person at Orchestrate reads it, and it is not used to train any AI or machine-learning model.

The one exception is when you choose to involve an agent. Orchestrate's agents are Claude Code, running under your own Claude account. If you hand an email thread to an agent, or an agent looks up your calendar to do something you asked, that content is sent to Anthropic as part of that agent's conversation, under your Claude account and Anthropic's privacy policy. This only happens because you asked for it. An agent handed your mail can only read and suggest: it can't edit files, reach the internet, or send anything.

If you connect other computers of your own to Orchestrate (over Tailscale), your data may pass between those computers. It still never reaches us.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Orchestrate's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other accounts

Microsoft (Outlook and Microsoft 365) and other email and calendar accounts you connect are handled the same way: Orchestrate talks to that provider directly from your computer, keeps the sign-in on your computer, and sends nothing to us.

Orchestrate runs the claude command-line tool you've installed, signed in with your own Claude account. What your agents send to Anthropic is covered by your agreement with Anthropic. Orchestrate also asks Anthropic for the list of available Claude models.

This website

orchestrates.dev uses no cookies, analytics or trackers. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may log visitors' IP addresses; see GitHub's privacy statement. The page asks GitHub for the latest release number to show on its download button. The app shown on the page runs entirely in your browser on made-up example data.

Children

Orchestrate is a developer tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. The history of every change is public in the project's repository.

Contact
